# Example: Vantage

This is a portable example brief for the video workflow. The prompt pack was written for reuse; it is not an exact replay of the development conversation.

## Product and scope

Vantage is a fictional AI financial research product for self-directed investors and active traders. It brings price movement, company news and participation into one research view.

Build a **marketing landing page selling this product**. A small product preview supports the story. Do not replace the assignment with a full trading dashboard.

- Primary action: explore a sample research brief; secondary action: preview early-access signup.
- Prototype: frontend only, with synthetic data and a local demo confirmation. No real account, payment or market feed.
- Suggested sections: hero, product proof, three research inputs, how it works, FAQ, final CTA.
- Headline: "Understand the move. See the context."
- Supporting copy: "Bring price action, company news and market participation into one clear research view."
- No invented returns, recommendations to trade, customers, testimonials or performance statistics.

## First round: three aesthetic directions

Supply your own accessible references for each. The names below describe the example choices; the agent must inspect the images rather than treating these summaries as evidence.

| Direction | Principles to investigate | A possible independent concept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product-led minimalism | Clear hierarchy, whitespace, strong product proof | A live research brief connecting the three inputs |
| Technical systems | Monumental type, image dominance, integrated annotations | A financial district with a research-input narrative |
| Quiet cinematic | Scale, atmosphere, restraint, broad negative space | A remote observatory suggesting perspective |

These concepts came from the local study. For a new product, derive new concepts from that product's promise. Viewers do not need our source images or generated assets to use the method.

## Second round: variations inside one direction

After choosing Technical Systems, for example, hold the reference pack, core concept and product story fixed. Explore three compositions: type-dominant, image-dominant and a balanced annotated layout. These are compositional alternatives inside one aesthetic, not three different skill treatments unless you explicitly choose that experiment.

## A practical video sequence

1. Show the brief and correct page type.
2. Open several actual reference screenshots and the carry-over/invent decisions.
3. Build three directions with one design approach; reveal them at equal sizes.
4. Show a too-literal or too-generic result and the specific correction when one occurs.
5. Pick a direction and compare three variations inside it.
6. Finish one variation, demonstrate mobile and the primary action.

The larger skill matrix can be a separate comparison segment. Keep its limitations visible: the original study used a continuing conversation and repeated revisions, not isolated randomized runs.
